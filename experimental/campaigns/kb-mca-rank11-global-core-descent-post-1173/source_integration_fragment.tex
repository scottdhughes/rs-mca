\begin{theorem}[Global-core descent payment at affine error rank eleven]
\label{thm:mca-kb-rank11-global-core-descent}
For the deployed KoalaBear row, after the intrinsic near-rational charge of
$134944$ slopes, every selected post-near affine-error-rank-eleven family has
at most
\[
 274980728111260143
\]
slopes.  Consequently the complete affine-error-rank-eleven branch contributes
at most
\[
 B_*=274980728111395087
\]
distinct bad affine slopes.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof interface]
For a selected family in row $(R+K,K,d+K)$ with direction rank $s$, complete
minimizing-pair core incidence yields a coordinate incident to at least
\[
 \left\lceil\frac{L(d+K)-C_s(K)}{R+K}\right\rceil
\]
slopes.  The incident pair-difference span is either proper, in which case
complete-agreement locator shortening drops the direction rank, or full, in
which case the coordinate is common to every selected minimizing pair and the
whole family shortens.  Exact deployed monotonicity reduces the recursion to
$K=s$, forcing $5201865$ slopes at rank one.  Weighted affine-line pair
counting bounds the rank-one endpoint by $4070947$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}
