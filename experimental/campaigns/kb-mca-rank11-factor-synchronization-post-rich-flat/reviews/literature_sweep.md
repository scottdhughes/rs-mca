# Targeted literature sweep

Date: 2026-08-15

The closest general combinatorial framework is dependent random choice, as
surveyed by Fox and Sudakov, which extracts subsets with large common
neighborhoods from dense incidence graphs.  The present theorem needs only
the elementary first-moment identities and discrete convexity, and obtains
exact finite integers rather than asymptotic graph-embedding parameters.

Guruswami--Kopparty strong subspace designs and folded-Wronskian methods remain
the closest algebraic neighbor for the successor step.  Their numerical
parameters apply to specially constructed subspace families and cannot be
imported for the received-line-dependent parent spaces here.

Sylvester/subresultant theory identifies a large polynomial gcd with rank
deficiency of structured resultant matrices.  That dictionary motivates the
next owner theorem, but no existing approximate-GCD or structured-low-rank
result supplies the required worst-case slope count with repository
chronology.

No external theorem is load-bearing.
