## Load extraction

The predecessor envelope is an upper bound on the disjoint union of near,
high-margin, anchor, and transverse row-space packets.  Subtracting it from
`B*+1` therefore gives a lower bound on the slope load in nontransverse
packets.  It is not a subtraction of unrelated upper bounds.

## Parent routing

A heavy proper flat `F<U^perp` gives `dim(F^perp)>=dim(U)+1`.  Choosing an
immediate intermediate parent is always possible.  The parent is frozen
deterministically, and coincident parents are merged.  Hence no residual slope
is duplicated.

All endpoint-difference rows assigned to a parent lie in that parent, so the
dimension-three interleaved pair cap applies to the merged load.  Using the
dimension-three cap for dimension-two parents is conservative.

degrees differing by at least two decreases `binomial(d,k)`.
