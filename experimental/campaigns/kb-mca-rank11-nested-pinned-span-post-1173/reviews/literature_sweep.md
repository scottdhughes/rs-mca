# Literature sweep

The closest primary source is Guruswami--Kopparty, *Explicit Subspace Designs*
(FOCS 2013; Combinatorica 2016).  Its Wronskian and folded-Wronskian arguments
bound intersections for specially constructed families of polynomial
subspaces.  The present immediate-parent spaces are selected adaptively from
one received line and do not satisfy that construction's hypotheses.

Guruswami--Xing and later function-field subspace-design work likewise concern
designed ambient families.  Those results motivate the next Wronskian attack
but do not supply the nested weighted flag or any numerical bound used here.

The load-bearing arguments in this packet are elementary exact double
counting, the inherited Reed--Solomon affine list theorem, and the inherited
sub-square interleaving collapse.
