# Controls

## Positive controls

1. **Same selected universe.**  The near, high-margin, anchor, transverse, and
   nontransverse packets are one disjoint partition at the selected cutoff.
2. **Immediate parents.**  Every nontransverse row space is routed to exactly
   one deterministic dimension-two or dimension-three parent; equal parents
   are merged.
3. **Exact common-zero sets.**  One canonical `q=36,776` subset is selected
   from each parent's actual anchor-zero set.
4. **Nested conditioning.**  The step-`k` parent family is a subset of the
   step-`k-1` family.  The resulting spaces satisfy `V_k<=V_{k-1}`.
5. **One owner multiplier.**  The factor `n-A=983,041` is applied per distinct
   pair type only after all parent spaces in a pin have been summed.
6. **Actual field.**  Every projected list cap through dimension ten has square
   below `2130706433^6`.

## Negative controls

- Do not replace `B*+1-total` by `B*-total`.
- Do not choose the coordinate pins independently.
- Do not infer that `V_k` itself is represented by one minimizing pair.
- Do not multiply a per-parent cap by the number of parents after forming
  `V_k`; use the one pair-list cap of the summed space.
- Do not cancel the locator separately for different parents.
- Do not book any active-v4 ledger movement.
