- Supports meeting the actual full common core are assigned to one rank-one
  owner.
- Supports avoiding it are partitioned by the number of clean projective-fiber
  equations they satisfy.
- Each slope is assigned once, with deterministic tie breaking.
- Fixed-pair and correction-ray charges are never summed over overlapping
  slope populations.
- Ray-universal coordinates are cancelled before the ray count, and the
  large-clone and heterogeneous-pair alternatives are treated as mutually
  exclusive.

first-match ledger atom, and does not close KoalaBear. The cell `K=662,479`
remains an exact eighteen-slope method wall.
