# Literature sweep

Closest classical context: Ray-Chaudhuri--Wilson higher incidence matrices,
Frankl--Wilson restricted-intersection theorems, and Alon--Babai--Suzuki
multilinear-polynomial proofs.

Those results motivate the higher-incidence direction for the rank-14 wall,
but no external theorem is imported.  The load-bearing low-type inequality is
the displayed one-line Cauchy argument.

Johnson and spherical-code refinements were also reviewed as possible
successors at rank eight.
