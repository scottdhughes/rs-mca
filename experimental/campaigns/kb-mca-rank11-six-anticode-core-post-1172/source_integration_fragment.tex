\begin{theorem}[Five-anticode payment and six-anticode sparse-pair router]
\label{thm:mca-rank11-six-anticode-router}
Retain the post-near KoalaBear affine-error-rank-eleven hypotheses and the
fixed actual minimizing-pair selection of
\cref{thm:mca-weighted-pair-core-route-cut,
       thm:mca-rank-one-pair-anticode-router}.
At support-margin cutoff \(\tau=1798\), the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item If the low-margin minimizing pair types are covered by at most five
pairwise-rank-one coefficient-matrix anticodes, then
\[
 |Z_{\rm bad}|\le256951591393251779<B_*.
\]
The slack is \(18029136718143308\).
\item Suppose the low pair types admit a cover by exactly six maximal
rank-one anticodes and the line is over budget.  Then all six anticodes are
fixed-left with distinct endpoint directions.  Their fixed endpoints are the
six projections of one global degree-\(<K\) codeword pair \((p_0,p_1)\).
Moreover
\[
 \left|\supp(r_0-p_0)\cup\supp(r_1-p_1)\right|\le167813.
\]
Consequently, after the separately proved full-lift support prefix through
\(96150\), the six-cover residual has minimum direction support in
\[
 96151\le e_{\min}\le167813.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put \(A=m-1798=1114250\) and \(d=A-K=65674\).  The high-margin tail is at
most \(59265463111193010\).  For a fixed-left anticode with fixed endpoint
agreement set of size \(g=n-q\), the ordinary affine-span list theorem and
the outside-fixed-endpoint owner injection give
\[
 F(q)=1+q\left\lfloor
 \frac{\binom{K-q+10}{10}}{\binom{d+10}{10}}
 \right\rfloor.
\]
Its exact maximum is \(39537225656384765\), uniquely at \(q=95326\).
Together with the near charge, five copies give the stated bound.

An over-budget six-cover needs at least \(215715265000067134\) low slopes.
The fixed-right cap is \(8147918\), so every anticode is fixed-left.  After a
disjoint cover assignment, each of the six groups has at least
\(18029136718143309\) slopes.  The adjacent exact values
\[
 F(247518)=18029257843230307,
 \qquad F(247519)=18029105617115083
\]
force \(g\ge1849634\) for every fixed endpoint.

Same-direction maximal anticodes coalesce, because their fixed endpoints
agree on more than \(K\) coordinates.  Thus the six directions are distinct.
Every triple of fixed endpoint sets has intersection at least
\(1354598>K\).  Two endpoint directions reconstruct one codeword pair; the
third agrees with its projection on more than \(K\) points and hence
identically.  This synchronizes all six directions to one pair \((p_0,p_1)\).

Let \(H\) be the complete agreement core of that pair and put
\(e=n-|H|\).  Outside \(H\), one nonzero received-pair error vector belongs
to at most one of the six distinct direction kernels.  Writing
\(q_i=n-g_i\) gives \(\sum_iq_i\ge5e\).

Now set
\[
 G(q)=q\binom{K-q+10}{10},
 \qquad D=\binom{65684}{10}.
\]
The exact first and second differences show that \(G\) peaks at \(95326\),
is discretely concave through \(190651\), and is discretely convex and
decreasing afterward.  At \(e=167814\), split the six \(q_i\) by the number
above the concavity interval.  Balance the low entries and move all but at
most one high entry to a convex-interval endpoint.  Exhausting the resulting
endpoint/residual configurations, the largest possible value occurs at
\((q_i)=(139845)^6\) and is below the required low mass by
\[
 \frac{
 5039866042250644297697303907940552741600048679872
 }{
 7575576854420300947226509036769468677
 }>0.
\]
For larger \(e\), the feasible set only shrinks.  Hence \(e\le167813\).
Finally choose \(p_1\) as the direction gauge; minimum direction support is
at most the pair-error union support, and the imported prefix yields the
stated residual window.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual critical order-32 minimizing-pair core]
\label{thm:mca-rank11-critical-pair-core}
Under the same hypotheses, every over-budget line contains a set of
\(378013809\) distinct low-margin slopes and a 32-coordinate set \(C_*\)
such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \(C_*\) is contained in the complete minimizing-pair core of every
selected slope;
\item at least \(385\) distinct minimizing pair types occur;
\item after common-core cancellation on \(C_*\), the affine endpoint-variation
space has dimension at least three.
\end{enumerate}
A complete cutoff scan finds \(9675\) cutoffs with conclusion
\textup{(iii)}.  The identical averaging/pair-cap method cannot force a
33-coordinate core; its closest cell has \(203\) pair types against cap
\(262\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use cutoff \(\tau=3304\).  The low mass of an over-budget line is at least
\[
 L=242720949552398957,
 \qquad A=m-3304=1112744.
\]
Double count pairs \((\gamma,C)\) with
\(C\in\binom{H_{e_\gamma}}{32}\).  Some \(C_*\) is incident to at least
\[
 \left\lceil L\frac{\binom A{32}}{\binom n{32}}\right\rceil
 =378013809
\]
slopes.  One pair type owns at most \(n-A=984408\) slopes, so at least
\(385\) pair types share \(C_*\).

After cancelling and deleting \(C_*\), one has
\(n'-K'=K\) and \(A'-K'=64168\).  If endpoint variation had dimension at
most two, the ordinary affine pair list and sub-square interleaving collapse
would give at most
\[
 \left\lfloor\frac{\binom{K+2}{2}}{\binom{64170}{2}}\right\rfloor
 =267
\]
pair types, with \(267^2<2130706433^6\).  This contradicts \(385\).
The all-cutoff and 33-core assertions are the exact finite scans recorded by
the certificate.
\end{proof}
