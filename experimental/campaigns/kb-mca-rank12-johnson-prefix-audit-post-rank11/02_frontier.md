The new prefix removes pair-type abundance as the bottleneck at the displayed cell: the low-deficiency type count falls from `25,551,333,830,332` to `54,568,751`.

The remaining obstruction is the weighted high-margin tail. In the local slope cap,

```text
low-deficiency contribution       53,536,228,373,355
high-margin contribution       3,240,390,795,118,310
```

so the high tail is more than sixty times the low contribution.

The strongest next theorem is therefore an exact ambient-dimension barrier compiler. It must combine Johnson prefixes over many deficiency thresholds with the source-bound descent recurrence and prove every early rank-drop child paid. A single endpoint recurrence or a single threshold cannot do this.

A degree-four or Terwilliger-strengthened Johnson dual is useful only if it extends the certified prefix far enough into the margin tail; improving the already-small low prefix at one threshold will not close the branch.
