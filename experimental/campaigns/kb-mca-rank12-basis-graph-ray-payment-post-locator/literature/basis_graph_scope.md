# Literature scope

The only external concept used for orientation is the basis graph of a
matroid. Stephen B. Maurer, *Matroid basis graphs. I*, Journal of
Combinatorial Theory, Series B 14 (1973), 216--240,
DOI `10.1016/0095-8956(73)90005-1`, is a classical primary source on these
graphs.

The load-bearing connectedness fact is nevertheless reproved directly:
repeated basis exchange reduces the symmetric difference between any two
bases, giving a path in the basis graph.

The Exa search also reviewed current Reed--Solomon MCA work, including
Sunghyeon Jo's 2026 beyond-Johnson paper and Przemek Chojecki's shortening
bounds. None supplies the four-support synchronization, universal-core-aware
ray cap, or deployed numerical payment in this packet.
