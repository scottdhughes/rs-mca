difference lies in $W$ and is supported in four omission sets. The
$4r<R+1$ minimum-distance bound makes adjacent normalized differences equal.
The basis graph is connected by basis exchange, hence all basis differences
synchronize. Modulo their common direction, every vector second difference
vanishes, which is exactly one affine correction ray.
\end{proof}
