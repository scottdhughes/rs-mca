# Controls

## Positive controls

1. **Frozen minimizers.**  The minimizing pair attached to each record is
   selected once before the cutoffs `1795` and `1936`; otherwise the dense
   center need not belong to the later low-margin family.
2. **Dense center.**  The parent weighted-pair theorem is replayed exactly:
   weight `360132809`, at least `200632` records, and maximum compatible core
   deficiency four.
3. **Actual intersection.**  A fixed `1116044`-subset of the center core meets
   every low pair core in at least `133004` deployed coordinates.
4. **Container projection.**  A rank-one difference row space is assigned to
   one two-dimensional extension only when that extension has at least
   `117731` zeros.  Otherwise it remains a terminal line.  Every rank-two row
   space is its own initial two-plane.
5. **One-time slope count.**  Pair types are assigned to exactly one container
   before the `Q_1`, `Q_2`, and fixed-pair owner bounds are applied.

## Hostile controls

1. A large common zero set for one polynomial is insufficient: both endpoint
   differences must vanish there, so the object being routed is their row
   space `U_e`.
2. A three-dimensional subcode with many zeros is not claimed to own many
   slopes.  It is a structural terminal, not a payment.
3. The theorem does not sum charges after cancelling different edge factors.
4. The center pair is charged once separately; allowing it inside every
   container list only weakens the upper bound.
5. The field guard uses the sextic line field, although the resulting
   `Q_2=255` guard is far from tight.
6. No active-v4 owner or ledger atom is inferred from a local direction
   subcode.

## Finite exhaustive controls

The independent audit enumerates all multisets of six evaluation normals in
`F_3^2`.

- Under a zero-class cap one and line-class cap three, every terminal-line
  flat has at least `(6-2+1)(6-4+1)=15` ordered independent coordinate bases.
- Under a line-class cap three, every two-plane container flat has at least
  `(6-4+1)^2=9` ordered independent coordinate bases.

These controls test the exact multiplicity-sensitive basis argument, including
zero normals and repeated projective directions.
