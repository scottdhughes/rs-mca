# Targeted primary-literature sweep

Question: does existing literature provide the source-bound, same-received-line
support-flag theorem needed to replace the internal basis count?

Primary and near-primary sources inspected include:

- V. K. Wei, *Generalized Hamming Weights for Linear Codes*, IEEE Trans.
  Inform. Theory 37 (1991), 1412--1418.
- T. Helleseth, T. Klove, and O. Ytrehus, *Generalized Hamming Weights of
  Linear Codes*, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 38 (1992), 1133--1140.
- J. Britz, *Higher Support Matroids*, Discrete Mathematics 307 (2007),
  2300--2308.
- A. Barg, *The Matroid of Supports of a Linear Code*, and the associated
  support-weight/Tutte-polynomial framework.

These sources justify the standard language of subcode supports, generalized
weights, and annihilator flats.  They do not supply the required theorem with
all of the present quantifiers: one actual received line, one frozen
minimizing-pair selection, a dense pair-core center, interleaved pair types,
and first-match-safe slope projection.

Verdict: no external theorem is load-bearing.  The packet's two-level basis
count is self-contained.  The literature supports the interpretation of the
terminal as a higher-support/matroid flat, not its payment.
