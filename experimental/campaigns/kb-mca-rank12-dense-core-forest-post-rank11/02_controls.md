# Controls

## Positive controls

1. **Complete cores.** Pair-core intersections of distinct minimizing pairs
   are at most `K-1` by Reed--Solomon root counting.
2. **Prefix types.** The ordinary affine pair-list cap is used only while its
   square is strictly below the sextic line-field size.
3. **Plotkin branch.** The Cauchy--Plotkin formula is used only when its
   denominator is positive; otherwise it contributes no bound.
4. **Monotone closure.** Raw prefix caps are enlarged, never reduced, to form
   a nondecreasing prefix sequence.
5. **Resource coupling.** A deficiency-`delta` record consumes at least
   `delta` units of the pointwise margin resource.
6. **Dynamic maximum.** Whole-family and proper-span outcomes are joined by a
   maximum, because the proof does not choose which outcome occurs.
7. **Source-bound shortening.** Every shortening uses complete scalar
   agreement domains and refreezes minimizing pairs after the move.
8. **Skipped ranks.** If a proper span drops by more than one dimension, the
   upper-rank evasion ceiling remains valid and only weakens the conclusion.

## Hostile controls

1. Replacing the dynamic maximum by a minimum is rejected.
2. Treating `9,342,184` as forced is rejected.
3. Using a nonpositive Plotkin denominator is rejected.
4. Removing the sextic field guard is rejected.
5. Counting witnesses instead of distinct slopes is rejected.
6. Summing loads from different forest leaves is rejected.
7. Promoting the rank-two endpoint calibration to an unconditional payment is
   rejected.
8. Any rank-twelve payment, KoalaBear closure, or active-v4 movement claim is
   rejected.

## Sharp adversarial terminal

At the full-code rank-two endpoint, the declared local inequalities permit an
abstract fifteen-star set system:

- fifteen disjoint cores of size `m-1=67,473`;
- `9,210,493` low-margin records distributed as fourteen stars of `614,033`
  and one star of `614,031`;
- singleton exceptions reused across different stars but disjoint within each
  star.

This is not asserted to be an actual Reed--Solomon received line. It proves
that pair-type count, resource, bounded intersections, and fixed-pair
exception packing alone do not contradict the terminal.
