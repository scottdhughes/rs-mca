# Independent mathematics audit

Status: **GREEN for the complete-agreement shortening theorem and its
application to the nested packet.**

## Support semantics

The original exact bad support lies inside the complete scalar agreement
domain.  If a codeword pair explained the complete domain, it would explain
the original support, so complete-domain pair noncontainment is valid.

The proof never claims that arbitrary replacement of coordinates preserves
badness.  The `GF(5)` control shows that claim is false.

## Shortening map

The interpolation pair is a genuine degree-`<K` codeword pair.  Every selected
scalar explanation minus its interpolated affine combination has the locator
as a polynomial factor.  Received words are divided only on undeleted
coordinates, where the locator is nonzero.

Any shortened simultaneous explanation lifts to degree `<K`, agrees on the
undeleted complete domain, and agrees on the deleted coordinates by
interpolation.  This contradicts original complete-domain pair
noncontainment.

## Nested compatibility

One degree-`<10` interpolation pair on `T_10` works for all prefixes.
Direction-space locator division is injective.  The relation
`(X-x_(k+1))*V'_(k+1) <= V'_k` follows directly from nested divisibility.

## Scope

The result supplies genuine shortened MCA families but no additive owner,
ledger movement, or rank-eleven payment.  The three unchanged row gaps explain
why shortening alone is not a numerical closure.
