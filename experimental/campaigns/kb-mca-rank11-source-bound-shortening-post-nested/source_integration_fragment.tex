\begin{theorem}[Complete-agreement locator shortening]
\label{thm:mca-complete-agreement-shortening}
Let a family of finite slopes be support-wise MCA-bad for
$\operatorname{RS}_{\mathbb F}(D,K)$ at agreement $m$.  Suppose each selected
scalar explanation has a complete agreement domain containing one fixed set
$T\subset D$, $|T|=k<K$.  Then, after subtracting a codeword pair
interpolating the received pair on $T$, dividing by the locator of $T$, and
deleting $T$, every selected slope remains support-wise MCA-bad for
$\operatorname{RS}_{\mathbb F}(D\setminus T,K-k)$ at agreement $m-k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Source-bound nested pinned-span ladder]

The ten pinned families in Theorem~\ref{thm:mca-rank11-nested-pinned-span}
are actual support-wise MCA-bad families in rows
$(2097152-k,1048576-k,1116048-k)$, with the same certified slope loads and
direction-dimension floors.  Their quotient direction spaces satisfy
$(X-x_{k+1})\widetilde V_{k+1}\le\widetilde V_k$.
\end{corollary}
